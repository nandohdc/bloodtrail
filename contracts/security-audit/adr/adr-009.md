## Decision

From 2.1.0 a record may carry one optional client_evidence reference: a ClientEvidenceEnvelope v1 interaction identifier, interaction kind, and envelope version, with provenance fixed to client_reported, client_claim, web_browser, and interaction_ephemeral. The envelope itself stays a separate contract and is never embedded.

## Consequences

- Absence carries no security meaning, and server-only records remain valid unchanged.
- The reference is a correlation annotation; it never overwrites, elevates, or evidences a server-owned field.
- A risk model that lists a client-evidence input is floored at client_claim by the derived-field trust floor.

## Unresolved Implementation Choices

- Mode A client origin is unverified and relayed by an authenticated producer; nothing in the contract proves a browser existed or that the producer did not fabricate the reference.
- Device-originated trust above client_claim requires a separate Mode B decision; until one exists no platform other than web_browser is admitted.
